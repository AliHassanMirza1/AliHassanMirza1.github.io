      Touch

      Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to steer and accelerate. Hold BOOST or DRIFT on the right. Tap a floating label to drive there automatically.

      Gamepad

      Left stick steers, RT accelerates, LT brakes, A enters, X boosts, B drifts.

      Tip

      Each light beam marks a stop. Park inside the glowing ring on the road and it opens on its own. You can also drag the view to look around.
